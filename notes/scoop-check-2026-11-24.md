# Scoop check, 2026-11-24 (before starting)

Question: has anyone reported whether OGLE-BLG182.1.162852 dimmed again in 2018 (or 2021, 2025) as
Rattenbury et al. (2015, arXiv:1410.2643) predicted, or measured its eclipse depth in the near-infrared?

Searches (2026-11-24):
1. Web search "OGLE-BLG182.1.162852 eclipse 2018": only the discovery paper (arXiv, MNRAS Letters 447, L31).
2. OpenAlex citations of doi:10.1093/mnrasl/slu176 (W1966302853): 21 citing works. None is a follow-up
   of this system; they cite it as one of the known disk-eclipse systems (J1407 ring models, V409 Tau,
   EE Cep, ASASSN-24fw, VVV-WIT-08 context, R Aqr, ELHC 10, ZTF circumbinary-occultation search, ...).
3. Closest: Hu, Zhu, Wang & Wang 2026, "Six New Circumbinary Disk Occultation (CBO) Candidates from the
   Zwicky Transient Facility", arXiv:2601.16828 (ApJS 2026). Table 2 lists OGLE-BLG182.1.162852 as CSO,
   P ~1277 d, duration ~100 d, from the discovery paper; it does not analyse any light curve of it or
   report any eclipse after 2014. (The target has no ZTF light-curve entry within 10"; checked via the
   IRSA ZTF light-curve API.)
4. Web search on VVV disk-eclipse giants: VVV-WIT-08 (Smith et al. 2021, arXiv:2106.05300) and the VVV
   most-variable sample (Lucas et al. 2024, arXiv:2401.14471) select dKs > 4 mag; neither names this
   system. Guo et al. 2024 (arXiv:2401.14464) does not mention it.
5. Gaia Science Alerts: the public alert table is too large to scan here; the job does not depend on it.

Conclusion: no report of the 2018/2021/2025 returns and no near-infrared depth were found. Re-check the
arXiv listing, Google Scholar and the OGLE / Rattenbury / Wyrzykowski pages again right before publishing.
